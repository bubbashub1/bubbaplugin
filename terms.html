Terms of Use

Clear, simple information about using Bubba Hub.

Using Bubba Hub

Bubba Hub provides a directory and planning service for families, groups, classes and community services. Information about activities, venues, prices, availability and booking arrangements may be supplied by organisers or other sources and can change.

Activity information

Families should check the organiser's latest information before attending, particularly for prices, dates, age requirements, accessibility, cancellations and safety arrangements. A listing on Bubba Hub does not by itself mean that Bubba Hub is the organiser of the activity.

Bookings and reservations

Where Bubba Hub provides a booking or reservation function, the information you submit is used to action that request. Where an organiser uses an external booking service, you may be sent to that service and its own terms and privacy information will apply.

Family and child information

Only provide child, medical, accessibility or emergency information when it is relevant to a booking or service and you are comfortable sharing it. Review the booking consent information before sending details to an organiser.

Accounts

You are responsible for keeping your login details secure and for providing accurate account information. Bubba Hub may suspend access where necessary to protect the service, users or organisers.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the service develops. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to contact@bubbahub.co.uk.
