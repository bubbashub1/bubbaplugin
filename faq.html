Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about using Bubba Hub, finding activities, accounts, bookings and more.

How does Bubba Hub work?

Browse local family activities, save favourites, plan your week and follow booking instructions from activity organisers.

Can I create a family account?

Yes. Your account can keep your family details, saved activities, preferences and planner in one place.

I'm an organiser. Where do I start?

Visit the Leader area to manage your profile, listings, schedules, bookings, payments and organiser FAQs.

Need more help?

Use the family Help area for practical guidance about using Bubba Hub, or Family Support for family-life information and support.
