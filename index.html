What We Build Together

Navigating early parenthood and finding trusted local support across Devon and Cornwall shouldn’t feel like an isolating maze, and you certainly shouldn’t have to figure it all out alone.

Bubba Hub exists to bring families, local baby groups, and community services together in one welcoming place—reducing parental isolation and making everyday life feel more connected, supported, and a whole lot easier.

A way of organising your family days that truly sustains you for the long run—powered by our trusted local directory, weekly planners, and seamless bookings—helping you feel more balanced, focused, and at ease.

Through simple, meaningful connections with local groups and leaders, you’ll gently return to a supportive community rhythm, creating small daily rituals that bring warmth, inclusion, and true connection to your parenting journey.

The person behind the screen

Hi, I’m Gina. I’m a mum on a mission to help families build a connected, supported life that actually feels good.

Bubba Hub exists because as a mum of two, I know firsthand how difficult it is to find groups and manage your week. I wanted to change that—bringing families, local baby groups, and community services together in one welcoming, connected place to make everyday family life feel supported and a whole lot easier.

Through our trusted local directory, weekly planners, and seamless bookings, we help you build a calmer, steadier family rhythm that truly sustains you for the long run. It’s all about creating small daily rituals and meaningful local connections that bring warmth, inclusion, and true community to your parenting journey.
