Why Bubba Hub?

Everything family life needs, in one place.

Bubba Hub brings discovery, planning, bookings and local community together — so families can spend less time searching and more time enjoying their week.

What is Bubba Hub?

Bubba Hub is an all-in-one family platform that connects parents and carers with local groups, classes, activities and family services. It brings finding, saving, planning and booking together in one simple place.

How does finding activities work?

Search and filter activities by location, town, age range, category, day and cost. You can switch between list and map views to discover what is available nearby.

Can Bubba Hub help me organise our week?

Yes. The Family Planner helps you build a weekly routine around the people, activities and plans that matter to your family. Saved activities and bookings can sit alongside your plans.

Can I book activities through Bubba Hub?

Where a group offers online booking through Bubba Hub, you can see available sessions and ticket options and book directly. Payment options can include secure providers such as Stripe or PayPal, depending on the organiser.

Is Bubba Hub just a directory?

No. The directory is the starting point. Bubba Hub is designed to help families discover local activities, save favourites, plan their time, manage bookings and stay connected with the groups and services they use.

Who is Bubba Hub for?

Bubba Hub is for parents, carers and families across Devon and Cornwall, from pregnancy and the early years through childhood. It is built around real local family life.

Can I save activities for later?

Yes. Save activities you like to your Bubba Hub so you can easily return to them when you are ready to decide or add them to your plans.

Can I find activities on a map?

Yes. Switch to map view to see activities and venues geographically, making it easier to discover options close to home or in a place you are visiting.

Can I find support services as well as activities?

Yes. Bubba Hub is designed to include local family support, community services and useful resources alongside groups, classes and activities.

Will there be a Pro account for families?

Yes. Bubba Hub is being designed to offer an optional Pro account for parents and carers, with enhanced family tools and features alongside the core Bubba Hub experience. The exact benefits and pricing will be clearly shown before you choose to upgrade.

Will there be a Pro account for group leaders?

Yes. Local groups, classes and activity providers will be able to access an optional Pro account with additional tools to help manage their Bubba Hub presence, activities and bookings. Features and pricing will be clearly explained before subscribing.

Can group leaders join Bubba Hub?

Yes. Local group leaders and providers can have a presence on Bubba Hub so families can discover their sessions, information and booking options.

Can I suggest a local group or activity?

Absolutely. Local recommendations help us build a useful, community-led directory and make it easier for families to discover great things happening nearby.

How do I get help or get involved?

Whether you are a parent, group leader, local service or community organisation, you can get in touch with the Bubba Hub team and start a conversation.
