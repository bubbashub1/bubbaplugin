Newsletter delivery

In-house service: /api/newsletter.php, scheduled job /cron/newsletter.php. Subscribers can select daily, weekly or monthly updates. Sending is handled by the configured server-side schedule.

Delivery status and subscriber totals require a dedicated authenticated reporting endpoint; they are not shown here yet.

Subscriber sign-ups

Newsletter registrations are handled by the in-house /api/newsletter-signup.php service. Existing subscriptions are managed through the standalone Bubba Hub system.

This tool currently provides service information, not a subscriber export or editing interface.
