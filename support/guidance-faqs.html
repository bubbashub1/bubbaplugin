📚 Guidance & FAQs

Quick answers about Bubba Hub, private support and finding trusted local family activities.

Is Bubba Hub a medical advice service?

No. Bubba Hub is a family community and activity platform. Information on these pages is general guidance and is not a diagnosis or substitute for professional advice.

Are private questions public?

No. Questions sent through the private support area are intended to stay between the family and the responder helping them.

Who can respond?

Questions can be connected with opted-in, verified Bubba Hub contributors where the relevant support area is available. Any relevant role or credentials should be shown.

Can leaders publish FAQs?

Yes. Class leaders can create public FAQs about their own classes, separate from private family questions.

How do I find local activities?

Use the Bubba Hub directory to search activities, groups, classes and services across Devon & Cornwall.

What should I do in an emergency?

Do not wait for a Bubba Hub response. Contact the appropriate emergency or professional service.
