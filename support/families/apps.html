Help & Support for Families

Suggested apps

Useful apps and digital resources we've come across that may help with family life, health, parenting and finding things to do.

Our suggestions

A few useful places to start

These are independent services, not Bubba Hub products. Always check the app's current privacy information, age requirements and terms before using it.

🏥

NHS App

Manage a range of NHS services, including appointments, prescriptions and other health services. Family and carer access may also be available through your GP surgery.

Visit NHS App
👶

Baby Buddy

A free pregnancy and parenting app with personalised information, development tracking, videos, care-planning information and wellbeing support.

Visit Baby Buddy
🎨

Hoop

Helps families find and book children's activities and things to do, from baby and toddler activities through to older children's events.

Visit Hoop
💬

Mush

A parent community app designed to help mums connect with other parents nearby, share experiences and find support.

Visit Mush
🩺

Baby Check

The Lullaby Trust's Baby Check app provides 19 simple checks for parents when a baby is showing signs of illness, alongside guidance about when to seek medical help.

Visit The Lullaby Trust

Please remember

Apps are not a substitute for professional help

Bubba Hub does not provide medical advice through this page. If you are worried about your child or yourself, use the appropriate NHS or emergency service, or speak to a qualified health professional.
