Using Bubba Hub

Finding activities

Use the directory to search by keyword, location, age, day and other filters. You can open an activity to see its details, venue and organiser information.

My Hub

My Hub helps you keep track of saved activities, family details and plans in one place.

Planner & calendar

Use the planner and calendar tools to organise family activities and see what you have coming up.

Account & sign-in

Use your account area to manage your Bubba Hub account. If you cannot sign in or something is not working, contact Bubba Hub support.
