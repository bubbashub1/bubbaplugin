About Bubba Hub

Bubba Hub is a family-focused directory built to make finding local activities, classes, events and support simpler for parents and carers across Devon and Cornwall.

Find activitiesMeet the team

💚 Our mission

To help every family feel more connected to their local community by making it easier to discover welcoming, accessible activities and useful support in one place.

We want parents and carers to spend less time searching and more time enjoying opportunities with their children.

🌱 Why we exist

Finding the right baby group, toddler class, family day out or support service can mean checking lots of different websites and social pages. Bubba Hub aims to bring that information together so families have a clearer place to start.

🤝 Supporting local communities

We want to celebrate local class leaders, organisers, small businesses and community groups, helping families discover what is available nearby.

✨ Growing together

We're developing Bubba Hub with the help of local people and volunteers. Our goal is to keep improving listings, share useful information and expand into more communities over time.

Get involved

Whether you're a parent looking for ideas, a class leader wanting to reach families, or someone who would like to volunteer, there's a place for you in the Bubba Hub community.
