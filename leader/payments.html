💳Bubba Hub bookings

Payments are designed to stay simple

When Bubba Hub booking payments are enabled for your account, families can reserve and pay through the booking flow linked to your class.

  • See reservations in your leader portal.
  • Keep your class price and booking details up to date.
  • Use your own booking website when you prefer external booking.
💚

Bubba Hub booking fee

The planned Bubba Hub booking system charge is 0.5% per booking when Bubba Hub handles the booking payment.

For example, a £5 booking would have a 2.5p Bubba Hub booking charge.

🔗

External booking

You can continue using your existing booking provider. Add the booking URL to each class so families can follow the correct route.
