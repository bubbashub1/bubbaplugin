Cookie Policy

Draft for legal and technical review — 19 October 2026. A complete technical cookie audit is required before publication as final.

Bubba Hub uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies. Some are needed to provide secure accounts, remember consent choices and operate essential features. Other technologies may require your permission before being set or accessed.

Essential technologies

These may support authentication, session protection, fraud prevention, booking checkout and storing your cookie preferences. Where an exemption applies, consent is not required, but we still explain their purpose.

Optional technologies

Analytics, advertising, personalisation and non-essential third-party embeds must follow applicable UK privacy and electronic communications rules. Optional technologies requiring consent should remain disabled until you choose to allow them. Refusing optional cookies should not prevent access to ordinary site content.

Manage your preferences

You can accept, reject or customise optional cookie categories through the cookie controls and reopen them using the Cookie settings button below. You can also clear cookies through your browser; this may sign you out.
