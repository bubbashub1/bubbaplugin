HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is the Expo free to attend?

Yes. General entry is free. Optional paid ticket options may be added later, but aren't available yet.

Do children need their own place?

The planned registration system lets you include adults and children in one booking. Registration is not open yet.

Can I come if I'm pregnant or have a newborn?

Absolutely. The event is designed around pregnancy, babies, young children and family support.

Where is the event?

Acorn Centre, Torquay, on Saturday 9 January 2027 from 10:30am until 2:30pm.

Can my business exhibit?

Family-focused businesses and organisations can review the exhibitor application. Stall applications are free, with an optional £20 contribution towards venue hire.

Will there be talks and taster sessions?

These are planned, with the final programme and timings to be announced once confirmed.
