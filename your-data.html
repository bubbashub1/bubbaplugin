This page explains how to exercise your UK data protection rights. Bubba Hub is operated by Bubba Hub, an unincorporated association. Contact contact@bubbahub.co.uk with the subject “Data request”. [CONFIRM: dedicated privacy contact and postal address.]

What you can request

You can ask whether we hold your personal data and request a copy; ask us to correct inaccurate details; request deletion where applicable; restrict or object to certain processing; and request portability where the legal conditions apply. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Not every right applies to every record.

Family and children's data

Family profiles may contain children's ages or dates of birth, preferences, saved activities and planner information. A parent or guardian can request review or deletion of family profile information, subject to identity and authority checks. We will not disclose a child's information to someone who cannot demonstrate appropriate authority. [VERIFY: actual child profile fields and any health/accessibility fields.]

Bookings, events and payments

Expo registrations, exhibitor applications, QR check-ins, sponsorship enquiries and payments may create records needed for event administration, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting or legal obligations. Removing your website account may not remove these records immediately. Card details are generally processed by payment providers rather than stored in Bubba Hub account profiles. [VERIFY: actual processor flows, data retention and records shared with exhibitors/organisers.]

How to make a request

Email us from your registered address where possible, describe the data or account concerned and the right you want to exercise. Do not email identity documents unless we request them using a secure method. We may ask for proportionate identity or parental-authority verification.

We normally respond within one calendar month. Complex or multiple requests may permit an extension of up to two additional months, and we will explain any extension. Requests are generally free, subject to limited legal exceptions.

Deletion and backups

We assess deletion requests against legal retention requirements and other exceptions. Where applicable, data may remain temporarily in secured backups until their scheduled rotation. [CONFIRM: backup rotation period, deletion implementation and retention schedule.]

Cookies and marketing

Use the Cookie settings control below to review optional cookies. Marketing consent can be withdrawn through the unsubscribe link in applicable emails or by contacting us.

Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
